# SECTION 4 — FANTASTICO SA — PHASE 1 COST ESTIMATE

## Scope of Work (As Per Addendum A)

Fantastico SA is commissioned to fabricate the **Fruitful Portal™ shell** — the complete branded exterior structure — excluding all technology, internal shopfitting, plumbing fixtures, and electrical wiring. Their scope covers:

1. Full fiberglass/composite cylindrical shell (approx. 1,810mm bottom dia × 2,050mm roof dia × 2,630mm height)
2. Coral-red domed top cap (fiberglass, moulded)
3. Mint-green body exterior (painted, smooth finish, weatherproof)
4. Fruitful™ logo & brand application (vinyl wrap or painted/moulded)
5. Tagline text application on body
6. 1 × lockable service/access door with stainless hinge and lever handle
7. Window/display aperture (glass or perspex panel, no tech)
8. Internal electrical conduit trunking (pre-routed channels, no live wiring)
9. Internal water inlet & drain channel routing (trunking only, no plumbing)
10. Ventilation grille mounting points
11. Fruitful™ branded signage topper (illuminated sign housing)
12. Base/plinth and levelling feet
13. Delivery to Johannesburg (Phase 1 — 1 prototype unit)
