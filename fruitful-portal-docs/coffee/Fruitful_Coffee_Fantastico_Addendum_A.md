## What I Am Asking You to Fabricate

I need Fantastico SA to fabricate the **complete Fruitful Portal™ physical shell** — the full branded exterior structure — for **Phase 1: one prototype unit**, with a view to a production run of 10–50+ units thereafter.

This scope **excludes** all internal technology, internal shelving/shopfitting, and live electrical or plumbing work. Those are handled by separate specialist trades. Your scope is the portal as a **physical art object and branded architectural structure**, with infrastructure channels built in and ready for others to fit.

### Scope Includes

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Fiberglass shell | Cylindrical body — approx. 1,810mm bottom dia × 2,050mm roof dia × 2,630mm height, 4mm fiberglass |
| Coral-red domed top cap | Moulded fiberglass, colour-matched to Fruitful™ brand coral red |
| Mint-green body finish | Full exterior paint, smooth weatherproof finish |
| Fruitful™ logo application | Moulded or vinyl logo + tagline on body (brand assets supplied) |
| Branded signage topper | Illuminated housing only, flush-mounted |
| Service/access door | 1 × lockable door, hinged, lever handle, colour-matched |
| Display aperture | Glazed or perspex window panel, flush fit, no tech |
| Electrical conduit trunking | Pre-routed internal channels for power and POS cabling, no live wiring |
| Water trunking channels | Internal routing for cold/hot water inlet and drain outlet, no plumbing fixtures |
| Ventilation provisions | Grille mounting openings for electronics heat dissipation |
| Base/plinth + levelling feet | Structural base suitable for indoor and outdoor placement |
| Phase 1 delivery | Delivery of 1 completed prototype unit to Johannesburg |

### Excluded From Scope

- Touchscreens, monitors, or digital interface hardware
- Internal shelving, product display fixtures, or refrigeration
- Plumbing fixtures, boilers, pipes, or final connections
- Live electrical wiring, DB boards, or electrical connections
- POS, modems, or network hardware
- Software or connectivity components
