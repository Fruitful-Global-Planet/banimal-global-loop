## 1. Brief introduction

This document is the definitive per-unit cost model for **all 10 menu SKUs** of the
Fruitful Portal radical menu. For every item it exposes the full stack, line by line:

> Ingredients → Packaging → **Direct cost** → **Selling price** → **Direct margin** →
> Shared & royalty layers (payment 3%, royalty 6%, marketing 4%, labour share, fixed share) →
> **Fully loaded cost**

**Fixed assumptions (identical for every SKU):**

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Labour (portal) | R16,800 / month | 2 baristas × R7,500 + 12% (UIF/SDL/leave) |
| Labour share / unit | **R5.89** | R16,800 ÷ 2,850 monthly units |
| Fixed share / unit | **R4.24** | R12,082 ÷ 2,850 (mall rent + Jura + opex) |
| Manager | R10,000 / month | Paid by HQ from the royalty + marketing pool — NOT portal labour |
| Payment fee | 3% of selling | Half-up rounded to 2dp |
| Royalty | 6% of selling | Franchisor % fee (McDonald's 4–5%, Starbucks licensed 6–8% of sales) |
| Marketing fund | 4% of selling | National campaigns |
| Baseline volume | 2,850 units / month | Coffee 1,500 + Smoothies 750 + Pancakes 600 |

**Monthly context (store P&L at these prices):**
Turnover **R112,500/mo** · Direct COGS R42,966 · Gross profit R69,534 (61.8%) ·
Labour = **14.9% of turnover** (24.2% of gross profit, 39.1% of COGS, 19.4% of fully
loaded R86,473) · EBITDA R26,027 · Net R15,957.80/mo ($985.93) · Break-even ~1,499
units/mo ≈ 50/day.

## 3. Phases

1. **Full-menu era** — 18-department BOQ, full menu with sizes, R38/R55/R35 pricing,
   pannekoek R35 and 12-pack R420. Flaw found: 12-pack was costed on the wrong
   (fluffy) pancake → corrected to the traditional SA 2-2-2 recipe.
2. **Radical-menu doctrine** — McDonald's-style 90% cut: 8 lines, no S/M/L/Grande,
   one price per category, prepack = zero close-of-day waste, one spec per SKU
   (Mercedes part-number discipline, FFP-01…09).
3. **Pricing finalisation** — 60%-margin alignment on average coffee cost; rejected
   R92/R38 attempts; final: coffee **R40** (all 3), smoothies **R60** (all 3),
   milk pannekoek **R12.50** (70.3%), water 50% no-box **R1.90** (60.5%),
   12-packs **R150 / R22.50** (12 × single; water pack rounded).
4. **Labour correction** — manager R10,000 removed from portal labour → paid by HQ
   from the royalty + marketing pool; portal labour = 2 baristas **R16,800/mo**;
   per-unit share **R5.89** (2,850-unit baseline). Incorrect draft share R6.60
   identified and rejected.
5. **Per-unit full exposure (this document)** — the 4-SKU pancake table extended to
   all 10 SKUs: every ingredient, packaging, fee and share line shown; fully loaded
   computed as the sum of displayed values (half-up rounding, e.g. water 12-pack
   payment R0.68).
6. **Business-model rebuild** — the per-unit fixed-allocation presentation was
   withdrawn for the store P&L (it produced a -485% "margin" on the R1.90 water
   pancake — an allocation artifact). Replaced by menu-engineering contribution per
   unit + % fees (payment 3%, royalty 6%, marketing 4%) charged on store revenue +
   fixed costs at store level, matching how McDonald's (royalty 4–5% + ad ~2–4% of
   sales) and Starbucks licensed stores (6–8% of sales) actually charge. Delivered
   as a global model: ZAR + USD, 9-currency price map, and a labour benchmark vs
   Starbucks / McCafé / Dunkin' / Tim Hortons / Costa.
7. **Open items** — Neon review DB not yet synced to these corrected values
   (`menu_lines`, `financial_snapshot`, `history_log` still show the old R35/R420
   FFP-07/08 rows). Pending the owner's go.
