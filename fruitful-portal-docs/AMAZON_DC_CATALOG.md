## 3. Amazon mechanics

1. Fruitful lists every SKU at supplier cost (marketplace account: Fruitful Shops).
2. Suppliers deliver bulk to Amazon DC (PTA/JHB nodes; nearest-port staging for imports).
3. Franchisee orders via the portal tablet / Amazon — order assembled at DC, delivered nationwide.
4. Amazon fulfilment fee **5–10%** (7.5% modeled) → passed to franchisee as a delivery line on their monthly supply invoice.
5. Amazon payouts → Fruitful weekly; distribution margin + rebates retained; zero national warehousing.

## 4. What this replaces
- No Fruitful national fleet for consumables (NUCO = deployments only).
- No kiosk stockpiling (DC holds the 30-day pantry; portal holds 2–3 days).
- No middlemen: every SKU traceable at supplier cost + 20% + 7.5% delivery — the "no nonsense" rule applied to supply.
