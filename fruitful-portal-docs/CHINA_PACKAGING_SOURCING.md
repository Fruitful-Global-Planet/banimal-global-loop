## ⚠️ Data-verification caveat (read first)

- **Prices, MOQ, supplier years/ratings** below were captured from **live Alibaba.com search listings and supplier-profile pages** (retrieved 15 Aug 2026). These are *listed* prices — final quotes depend on print colours, material grammage and exact MOQ.

Where no public figure exists it is marked **⚠️ TO-CONFIRM** and given as an industry-typical range — **do not use these for the final container plan until the supplier confirms a packing list.**
- Nothing below is fabricated; every number is either sourced or explicitly flagged as unverified.

## 4. Branded Ceramic Mugs (~350 ml / 11 oz, Fruitful top-view print)

**Spec note (important):** "Top-view print" (logo visible looking *down into* the mug, i.e. on the rim / inner base / inner wall) is **not** standard decoration.
