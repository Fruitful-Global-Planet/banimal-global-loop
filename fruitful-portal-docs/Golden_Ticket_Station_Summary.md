**Base data (% of revenue):** Fruitful labour 14.9% / COGS 38.2% / gross 61.8% / fully loaded 76.9% · Starbucks 26.5% / ~31.5% / 68.5% / 87.0% · McCafé-McDonald's 31% / ~28% / ~72% / ~89% · Dunkin' 28.0% / 28.6% / 71.4% / ~87.5% · Tim Hortons ~28% / ~30% / ~70% / ~88% · Costa ~27% / ~30% / ~70% / ~88% · Independent 25–30% / 25–35% / 65–75% / ~88%.

**The businessman read:**
- Fruitful labour = **14.9% of turnover vs 26–31% globally** — HALF the norm (2 staff, one till, 8 lines, no sizes, manager at HQ).
- Heavier ingredient bill (38.2% vs 28–32%) — real ingredients vs industrial supply chains; pay more for food, far less for people.
- Labour is 39% of COGS (global 84–111%) — the zero-waste trade, opposite weighting.
- Leanest fully-loaded structure (19.4% vs 30–35%) — no store manager, no franchise fee paid out, Fruitful IS the franchisor.

*Sources: Starbucks wages/benefits 26.5% & store opex 55.5% of co-op revenue (FY25 investor release, stock-analysis-on.net); gross margin 68.6% (alphaquery/macrotrends) · McDonald's franchisee labour 31% (franchiseinvestordata), food cost 24.7% + paper (aprio), royalty 5% + ad ~4% (eatbreadless) · Dunkin' labour 28.0%, COGS 28.6% (2024 FDD via franchisechatter), royalty 5.9% + ad 5% (franchisedirect) · Industry labour 25–30% (embercoffee, taxfyle, lavu), COGS 25–35% (getharvest, bellwether), net profit 2.5–7% (vantainsights) · Tim Hortons & Costa: no public store-level P&L — industry-based estimates.*
