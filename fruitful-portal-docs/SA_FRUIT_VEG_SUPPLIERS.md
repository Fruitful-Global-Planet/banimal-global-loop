# Fresh Fruit & Veg Wholesale Suppliers — Pretoria / Gauteng (SA)

**Prepared for:** Fruitful Portal (SA Bush flagship kiosk, Pretoria)
**Use case:** Small kiosk-scale volumes — fruit bowls, smoothies, cold-pressed juices.
**Required items:** mango, pineapple, kiwi, strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, oranges, grapefruit, pomelo, watermelon, cucumber, fresh mint, baby spinach, green apple, ginger, dragon fruit, banana, lemon, lime, apples, beetroot, carrot.
**Research date:** 15 August 2026
**Geography:** Delivery to Pretoria preferred; Gauteng-wide acceptable.

---

## Executive Summary

Three suppliers are recommended for a small kiosk account, ranked for freshness, price, and ability to handle small volumes:

1. **Tshwane Market (Pretoria West) — direct wholesale buying via market agents** — lowest prices, zero minimum, 5 km from most of Pretoria; best for a daily/weekly walk-in purchase.
2. **Freshpro Market — delivered wholesale** — explicitly "no minimum order," R30 delivery (free over R400), WhatsApp ordering, serves Pretoria & Johannesburg, sources from Gauteng markets (fresh from the Tshwane/Joburg floors daily).
3. **Nasci Produce — Pretoria-based family wholesaler** — serves restaurants/schools/offices/retailers of all sizes, Gauteng-wide delivery, daily pricelist on request, prepped/ready produce (saves labour for smoothies & juice).

**Backup option:** De Farmer (defarmer.co.za) — online ordering at wholesale market prices, R300 minimum, free Gauteng delivery.

**Not recommended for this scale:** Freshmark (Shoprite's in-house procurement arm — supplies its own retail chains only), KingFresh (export/bulk focus — confirm minimums), Agri-Market (retail farm-shop chain, not a delivery wholesaler), "Biza" (could not be verified as a live SA fresh-produce platform).

## 1. Tshwane Market — RECOMMENDED (cheapest, most flexible)

**What it is:** The City of Tshwane's municipal fresh-produce market (formerly Pretoria Market) — the second-largest of SA's 16 national fresh-produce markets. Prices form daily by supply/demand and set the national price trend. All of Fruitful Portal's required items are traded here in season (including berries, citrus, tropicals, herbs, and spinach).

**Location:** 450 President Burgers Street, Pretoria West (close to the flagship's Pretoria catchment; DF Malanry Ave entrance used by agents).

**How small accounts buy:**
- Get a buyer's tag from the market chief cashier for **R10** (ID/passport + proof of residence).
- Open a **secured cash account** (deposit cash/EFT, recommended for small buyers) or a **credit account** (requires bank guarantee).
- Buy any quantity from any market agent — there is **no minimum order**. Agents sell from single crates/punnets up.
- Client service centre: Halls A & B, tel **012 358 2302**; general line **012 358 2398**.

**Trading hours:** Weekdays 05:00–10:20; Saturdays 05:00–09:20 (buy early — stalls sell out). Cashiers/collection until 13:00 weekdays, 11:00 Saturdays.

**Representative agents on the Tshwane floor** (registered with APAC):
- **Noordvaal Market Agents** — 012 323 2229, noordvaal.co.za (general fruit & veg, potatoes)
- **Botha Roodt Pretoria (trades as Fresh Way)** — 012 321 5371, charlotte@brpta.co.za / louis@freshway.co.za
- **Subtropico Tshwane (Protea Market Agency)** — 012 460 9910, attie@subtropico.co.za — markets 200+ products incl. tropicals (mango, dragon fruit) and berries
- **DW Fresh Tshwane**, Fresh Way (Maupa Seseni 083 400 7543), and others listed via APAC

**Price list availability:** No fixed list — daily market prices published via the market's info system and agents' daily sheets; typically the cheapest rates in Gauteng. Indicators (see §6).

**Kiosk fit:** Excellent — lowest prices, zero minimum, full range, ~10 min from Pretoria CBD; requires early-morning transport and cash/EFT account. Best as the primary weekly "market run" + a delivered supplier for top-ups.

**Sources:** https://www.noordvaal.co.za/tshwane-market/ · https://www.agribook.co.za/fresh-produce-markets/ · https://apacweb.org.za/registered-fresh-produce-agents/

## 6. Wholesale Price Context (indicative, verify on the day)

South African wholesale prices are **seasonal and change daily** at the markets. Verified reference points (Aug 2026 report date):

| Item | Indicative wholesale (SA) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mango | ≈ R23–26/kg (US$1.25–1.38/kg, Feb 2026 transactions) | https://www.tridge.com/intelligences/mango/ZA/price |
| Blueberries | ≈ R177–200/kg (May 2026, high local prices); lower grades ~R60/kg (2022) | https://www.freshplaza.com/north-america/article/9841886/global-market-overview-blueberries/ · https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/agri-news/south-africa/farmers-losing-farms-as-price-of-blueberries-falls/ |
| Strawberries | ≈ US$5.65/kg export unit price (SA, Tridge); retail in SA typically R60–110/kg | https://www.tridge.com/market-overview/fresh-strawberry |

**How to lock real prices:** ask each supplier for their **daily pricelist** (Nasci emails daily; Freshpro sends WhatsApp catalogues; De Farmer posts live online prices; market agents quote on the floor). Berries and dragon fruit are the highest-cost, most volatile line — budget 3–4× the price per kg vs. citrus/banana/roots.

## 7. Recommended Procurement Strategy for Fruitful Portal (kiosk scale)

1. **Weekly bulk (lowest cost):** Buy at **Tshwane Market** every Monday/Wednesday morning (buyer's tag R10, cash/EFT account). Source mango, pineapple, citrus, watermelon, cucumber, ginger, beetroot, carrot, banana, apples in bulk — cheapest prices in Gauteng.
2. **Daily top-ups + berries/herbs (delivered):** Use **Freshpro Market** (WhatsApp before 9 am; no minimum; R30 delivery, free >R400) for strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, kiwi, dragon fruit, fresh mint, baby spinach — the fragile, low-volume, high-rotation lines.
3. **Optional prep/shortcut:** **Nasci Produce** for pre-cut fruit/veg and packaged herbs if in-store prep labour is tight; ask for their daily pricelist first.
4. **Fallback:** De Farmer online (R300 min, free Gauteng delivery) when both 1 & 2 are unavailable.

**Practical notes:** open a small wholesale credit account with 1–2 Tshwane agents (e.g., Noordvaal, Subtropico) so prices and supply are stable; confirm dragon fruit & raspberries availability with each supplier weekly (import-dependent, seasonal).
