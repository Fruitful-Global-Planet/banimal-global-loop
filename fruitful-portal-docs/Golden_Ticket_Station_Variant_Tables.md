**The corrected logic (pancakes):**
- **Milk 100%** — boxed single R12.50 + 12-pack R150 (70.3% direct margin)
- **Water 50%** — **no box** (that's the R1.25 saving — loose, street-style): single **R1.90** (60.5%) + 12-pack **R22.50** (60.0%)

- **The water 50% at R1.90 is the pure impulse weapon** — the price people don't think about; the milk 100% at R12.50 is the margin product. Two products, one recipe family, four SKUs.
